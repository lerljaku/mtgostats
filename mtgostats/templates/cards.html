My cards

A card counts as used in a game when it did anything for you: cast, played, discarded, cycled, triggered, had an ability activated, or attacked. WR when used is your game win rate in those games; Difference compares it with games in the current filter where the card was never used. Cast in shows how many of those games you actually cast or played it. WR discarded is your win rate in games where you discarded it, with the number of games in brackets - useful for reanimator and graveyard decks. WR in graveyard widens that to any way the log shows it in your graveyard: discarded, milled, sacrificed, put there by surveil and similar, or cast or returned from it. MTGO doesn't log creatures dying or spells resolving, so those don't count. {% if not filters.deck %}
